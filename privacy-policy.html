Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Daily IT Jobs Automation ("we", "our", or "the application") provides automated IT job posting and related social media functionality.

Information We Access

The application may access information necessary to publish job-related content to Instagram and to process comments and messages associated with our Instagram account.

How We Use Information

Information accessed through the Instagram platform is used only to operate the application's job-posting and comment-response functionality.

Instagram Data

We use Meta's Instagram APIs to publish job posts and process relevant Instagram interactions. We do not sell Instagram user information or use it for unrelated purposes.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the application's functionality, maintain logs, and respond to job-related requests.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application. Access credentials and API tokens are intended to be stored securely and are not included in publicly accessible source code.

Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services including Meta Instagram APIs and Cloudinary for functionality such as social media publishing and image hosting.

Data Deletion

If you would like to request deletion of information associated with your interaction with the application, please contact the application owner through the contact information provided on the application's associated website or repository.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the application owner through the GitHub repository associated with this application.
